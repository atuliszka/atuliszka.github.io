About the game

Brew, automate,
begin again

Potion Brewer: Alchemist Idle blends satisfying tap-to-brew action with workshop automation and a magical prestige system. Turn each drop into gold, improve brewing efficiency, and hire managers to keep the cauldron productive while you are away.

Unlock rare ingredients, experiment with recipes, and reset your workshop for Arcane Essence when progress slows. Spend it on permanent artifacts and speed boosts, then chase more than 35 achievements on the road to the Master Alchemist ending.
