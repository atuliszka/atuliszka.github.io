Game features

Grow your influence, keep your progress

Each promotion opens new possibilities. Earlier production systems keep contributing, with no prestige resets or rank demotion.

01

From hand to harvest

Begin as a serf farming by hand. Hire workers and automate production as your opportunities grow.

02

Trade the market

Watch changing prices, choose when to buy and sell, and reinvest in your enterprises.

03

Mine and forge

Expand into mining and arms production. Turn raw materials into weapons and grow your industry.

04

Reclaim the regions

Extend your influence through regional reclamation as the campaign opens new ground to explore.

05

Take to the seas

Fulfill maritime contracts and manage your fleet as sea trade becomes part of your growing economy.

06

March beyond the fields

Recruit an army, send land expeditions, and defend the town. Discover minigames later in your journey.
