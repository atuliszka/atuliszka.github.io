About the game

From one field
to a kingdom

Peasant to King is a cozy idle management game about turning a simple farm into a prosperous medieval estate. Plough fields, sow crops, harvest grain, and sell resources when the market is in your favor.

Invest your earnings in tools, buildings, storage, and production. Assign workers to automate the estate, continue earning while away, and progress across four regions as you rise from serf toward merchant—and a realm worthy of a crown.

Genre
Idle farming and management simulation
Platform

Play style
Single-player, cozy progression
