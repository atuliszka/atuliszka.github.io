About the game

Cut the land.
Rebuild the realm.

Kingdom Cutter: Cut & Conquer is a fast arcade strategy game with a classic territory-capture twist. Draw a line through unclaimed ground and reconnect to capture it—but stay exposed too long and roaming monsters can cut you off.

Bring resources home to recruit soldiers, forge stronger shields, improve your mount, and rebuild your medieval kingdom. Master the campaign or test your route planning and reactions across Ladder, Daily, and Legends challenges.
