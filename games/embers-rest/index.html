About the game

Grow by day.
Hold until dawn.

Embers Rest combines idle progression, village building, and wall-defense strategy. Gather gold, wood, food, and other resources to expand your settlement, research technologies, and prepare for the next attack.

When night falls, recruit archers, spearmen, catapults, ballistas, and more to survive escalating waves. Your village continues producing while you are away, so every return brings resources for the next round of upgrades.
